SAN ANDREAS BUREAU
PROJECT BLACKOUT — COMMUNICATIONS INTERRUPTION

Archive Reference: CM-6381
Record Date: 3 October 1992
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL
Status: Under Review

Several internal communications were reported missing from the departmental message register.

The interruption lasted less than one operational shift. No official explanation was recorded.

A technician noted that the surviving logs appeared complete, although their sequence numbers were inconsistent.

No further action was authorized.

Filed by: Communications Division
Department: Technical Records
