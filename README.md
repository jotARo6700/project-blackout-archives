Project Blackout Archives

San Andreas Bureau — Restricted Records Division

Welcome to the Project Blackout Archives.

This repository contains recovered documents, incident reports, maintenance logs, and fragments collected during an investigation into missing records across San Andreas.

Some files may be unrelated to the investigation. Others may contain details that were overlooked during the original review.

You may find many more such records on @blackout_archives56

Archive Notice

All documents have been preserved in their recovered form. Their accuracy and completeness have not been verified.

Investigators should compare records carefully before drawing conclusions.

Investigation Guidelines

- Review the available records.
- Follow references between documents.
- Look for information beyond the official archive.
- Verify clues before accepting a conclusion.

Some records are missing. The trail is not.
