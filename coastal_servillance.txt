SAN ANDREAS BUREAU
PROJECT BLACKOUT — COASTAL SURVEILLANCE

Archive Reference: CS-4728
Record Date: 8 September 1992
Classification: INTERNAL
Status: Closed

Routine surveillance along the western coastline recorded an unidentified vehicle near a restricted access road.

The vehicle was described as dark-colored, with no readable registration plate.

No direct connection to Project Blackout was established. The patrol report was filed for reference and closed pending further evidence.
